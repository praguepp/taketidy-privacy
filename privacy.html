TakeTidy Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026

TakeTidy edits talking-head videos on your iPhone. It's built so that your videos, your voice and what you say never leave your device. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and why.

The short version

  • We don't collect any personal data. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your videos, audio and transcripts are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details.

What happens to your videos

When you import or record a video, TakeTidy keeps a working copy inside the app's private storage on your iPhone. To find pauses, filler words and retakes, the app:

  • reads the audio track to measure loudness, and
  • transcribes the speech with Apple's on-device speech recognition (the Speech framework).

Transcription runs on your iPhone. The first time you use it, iOS may download Apple's speech model for your language. That download comes from Apple and contains no audio or video from you.

The working copy, the transcript and your edits stay on your device. The app keeps your 20 most recent projects; deleting a project, or deleting the app, removes them. Exported videos are saved where you choose (Photos or the share sheet) and are then managed by you.

We have no servers that receive your content, so we couldn't access it even if asked.

Data stored on your device

  • Projects: the working copy of each video, its transcript and your edits.
  • Preferences: your cut settings (strength, breathing room, filler words and so on).
  • Free export flag: a single yes/no value in your iPhone's Keychain that records whether you've used your free export. It contains nothing about you, and it stays in the Keychain if you reinstall the app so the free export can't be reset by reinstalling.

None of this is sent to us.

Purchases

TakeTidy Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your device whether you have an active purchase. We don't receive your Apple Account details or payment information. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't include your videos or transcripts. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

TakeTidy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device, and you can remove it at any time by deleting projects or the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes. If we ever wanted to collect data, we would ask for your permission first.
