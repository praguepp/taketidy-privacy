TakeTidy Support

TakeTidy cuts pauses, filler words and retakes out of talking-head videos, on your iPhone.

Contact

Email praguepp@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A short screen recording helps.

Best results

TakeTidy is built for one person speaking English to the camera, in a take up to 30 minutes. Continuous music, loud rooms and non-English speech usually produce few or no cuts.

Purchases

Your first export is free (up to 10 minutes). TakeTidy Pro is an auto-renewable yearly or weekly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open TakeTidy › Settings › Restore purchases.

Privacy

Videos and transcripts stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
